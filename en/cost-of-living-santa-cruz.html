First things first: the boliviano changed in 2026

From 2011 to June 2026, the dollar was officially worth 6.96 bolivianos. Since June 26, 2026, Bolivia has applied a flexible exchange rate system: the Central Bank publishes an official rate every day, which was 11.69 Bs to US$1 from October 17 to 19, 2026. The parallel market was at the same level, around 11.8 Bs.

In practice: an article or video that converts prices at 6.96 Bs gives outdated dollar amounts. To compare, rely on prices in bolivianos and the current rate.

Getting around

  • Bus: 3 Bs per ride (≈ US$0.26); monthly pass 270 Bs (Numbeo, Oct. 5, 2026).
  • Taxi: about 20 Bs base fare (≈ US$1.71); most rides in town are booked through ride-hailing apps.
  • Regular gasoline at the regulated price: 6.96 Bs per liter (≈ US$0.60), price frozen at least until January 12, 2027 (DS 5652 of July 9, 2026). But it is often in short supply: in early October 2026, the press again reported shortages and lines in Santa Cruz.
  • Imported gasoline: since September 11, 2026, private stations in Santa Cruz have been selling imported gasoline at market price, around 18 to 18.40 Bs per liter (≈ US$1.54 to 1.57), or about 2.6 times the regulated price. This price follows the international market and may change.
  • Premium gasoline: 11 Bs per liter (DS 5516).
  • Diesel: 17.95 Bs per liter (≈ US$1.54) since September 19, 2026 (DS 5716), nearly double the previous 9.80 Bs; price confirmed on October 7, 2026 (DS 5741).

Electricity, internet, phone

  • Electricity, water, air conditioning and garbage for an 85 m² home: 419 Bs per month on average (≈ US$36), with wide variations depending on air conditioning use (250 to 815 Bs, Numbeo). Since DS 5750 of October 13, 2026, electricity rates are adjusted monthly: this amount may change.
  • Fiber internet: 229 Bs per month for 100 Mbps with Tigo (≈ US$19.60, price list checked October 19, 2026).
  • Mobile plan with 10 GB or more: 229 Bs per month on average (Numbeo).
  • Gym membership: 231 Bs per month on average (Numbeo).

Healthcare

Healthcare prices are rarely published in Santa Cruz: the large private clinics do not post their rates. One published example: at Centro Médico Paraíso (Kolping Foundation), a consultation costs 85 Bs with a general practitioner (≈ US$7) and 90 Bs with a specialist (page updated August 17, 2026).

Bolivian insurers offer plans for older adults, for example "Salud Total Adulto Mayor" from Nacional Seguros (enrollment from age 60 to 79), but with no published premium. The right choice depends on your age, your health and your current coverage: compare before you leave.
