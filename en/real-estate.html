Understanding pre-construction

Buying "en pozo": what does it mean?

In Santa Cruz, many new buildings are sold before or during construction: this is called pre-construction buying, known locally as "en pozo".

  • You choose your floor, your view and your floor plan from all available units.
  • You pay in stages, following the developer's payment schedule, often until delivery.
  • You receive a brand-new home, with modern amenities and the guarantees set out in the contract.
  • You wait for delivery: the planned date, the guarantees in case of delay and the contract currency are checked before signing. That is precisely our role.

Frequently asked questions

Buying in Bolivia, in practice

Other questions? We answer them during the free call.

Can a foreigner buy an apartment in Bolivia?

Yes. A foreigner can buy an apartment in Santa Cruz without being a resident: the passport is the ID used at the notary. The Bolivian Constitution only prohibits foreigners from acquiring property within 50 km (31 mi) of the borders (article 262), which does not affect Santa Cruz de la Sierra. Depending on the notary, a Bolivian tax number may also be requested: we check this with you before signing.

How is a pre-construction purchase paid?

It depends on the developer: in cash, in installments during construction, or sometimes with direct developer financing. We compare the options with you, taking into account the currency and exchange rate set in the contract.

What happens if delivery is delayed?

It depends on the contract. That is why, before signing, we check the planned delivery date and the written guarantees in case of delay with our partner lawyer.

Can I rent out my apartment?

Yes. To rent legally, you need a tax number (NIT), which in practice is obtained with the foreign resident ID card, and therefore residency. We support you with both, and connect you with local property managers.

Do you give investment advice?

No. We present verifiable facts (prices, sizes, terms, sourced market data) so you can make an informed decision. We promise no returns and give no financial or tax advice.
