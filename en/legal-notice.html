Nature of our services

Résidence Bolivia assists its clients with their administrative procedures and coordinates them. Résidence Bolivia is not a law firm, an immigration authority, a financial advisor or a medical provider. Whenever professional advice or a professional act is required, it is carried out by independent professionals duly authorized in Bolivia (lawyers, notaries, official translators, accountants).

The timelines shown on this site are estimates based on experience; the relevant authorities set the pace of the process. Every engagement begins with a written service agreement that sets out its scope and price.
